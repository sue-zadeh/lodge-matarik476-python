Lodge Matariki No.476

About Us

A lodge created from a shared vision for renewal, unity and the future of Freemasonry in our district.

Who We Are

Lodge Matariki No.476 was formed following a period of consultation and cooperation between lodges across the district. The aim was to create a new beginning while preserving the history, fellowship and values built by previous generations.

The new lodge brought together members from Otahuhu No.387, Papatoetoe No.227, Manurewa No.222 and Tironui No.400, creating one lodge with a shared future.

Our Beginning

A New Lodge, Built on Strong Foundations

After years of consultation, planning and collaboration, a Petition for Charter was signed by 114 members across the district.

Lodge Matariki No.476 was officially Dedicated and Constituted on 19 October 2019 at the Jet Park Function Suite in Mangere.
